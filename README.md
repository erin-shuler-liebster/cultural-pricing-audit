# Cultural Pricing Communication Analyzer

This app helps businesses analyze their website pricing communication based on Hofstede's cultural dimensions. It reads the content of any webpage and compares it against cultural expectations for markets such as the US, France, Germany, Sweden, and more.

### What You Can Do:
- Paste your website’s sales page URL
- Select a target country
- Get analysis on what you’re communicating
- Understand what your audience perceives
- Discover possible actions the audience may take
- Receive recommended pricing communication improvements
- Export results to CSV or PDF

### Use Cases:
- Test your digital marketing strategy for cultural alignment
- Prepare market-entry strategies
- Validate your localization approach
- Replace guesswork in pricing strategy with research-based cultural diagnostics

### Based On:
- Hofstede's 6-D Cultural Framework
- Cross-Cultural Consumer Behavior research
- Pricing Communication Theory
- Digital Marketing Psychology

### Core Files:
- `streamlit_app.py`: main interface
- `website_audit.py`: page analysis logic
- `cultural_pricing_algorithm.py`: translation framework
